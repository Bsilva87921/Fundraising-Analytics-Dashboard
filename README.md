- Donation Trends by Year

## Key Insights

- Alumni Association generated the highest donation revenue.
- Credit Card and Stock gifts generated significant revenue.
- Fundraising revenue increased significantly in recent years.
- Major donors contribute a meaningful share of total fundraising revenue.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Expand alumni-focused fundraising initiatives.
2. Increase investment in digital giving channels.
3. Strengthen major donor stewardship.
4. Develop donor retention programs.

- Power BI

- Data Visualization

# Executive Summary

This project demonstrates the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze fundraising performance, donor behavior, donation trends, and giving channels using a large-scale donor dataset. The objective was to transform fundraising data into actionable insights and strategic recommendations that support data-driven decision-making.

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Expand Alumni-Focused Fundraising Initiatives
The Alumni Association generated the highest fundraising revenue, suggesting strong donor engagement within the alumni community and significant potential for future growth.

### 2. Strengthen Major Donor Stewardship
Develop targeted engagement strategies for high-value donors through personalized outreach, impact reporting, and relationship management programs to improve donor retention and lifetime value.

### 3. Continue Investing in Digital Giving
Strong performance from Credit Card and Online giving channels suggests opportunities to further optimize digital fundraising experiences and donor conversion rates.

### 4. Develop Donor Retention Programs
Future reporting should focus on donor retention, reactivation, donor upgrades, and donor lifetime value to support long-term fundraising sustainability.

### 5. Leverage Predictive Analytics and AI
Future enhancements will incorporate AI-powered forecasting and automated recommendations to predict fundraising outcomes, identify emerging donor trends, and proactively support fundraising decision-making.
